  only frozen hashes/model/counts, no credential/header/source contents.

  closures. data.stage({seal,forward}) gives opaque identity handle, detached

  forwarding. False/throwing claim, forged handle, duplicate request/release,
  uncertainty and late retry permanently poison FAILED; no refund or replay.
- createNativeResponseGuard({statusCode,requestId,model,maxTokens,subjectSha,
